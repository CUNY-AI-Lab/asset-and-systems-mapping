6. **Provisional meeting evidence** — the August 5 systems/assets notes are working minutes authored by Zach and remain unconfirmed by Maria.
7. **Architecture precedent** — the canonical STEM Splitter checkout informs hosting, model contracts, persistence, privacy, and validation. It does not determine this product's subject matter or visual metaphor.

Later direct user decisions supersede earlier specifications. The current progression is Micro, Meso, Exosphere, and Macrosphere. Micro identifies discrete elements in immediate settings. Meso adds relationship arrows between the same Micro nodes, including people and roles. Existing scoped Meso records remain available for review; new work uses relationships. Exosphere replaces Macro; Macrosphere replaces Integration. The latter two levels use nested, filterable ecological regions. Instructor review stays advisory, and no review result or outstanding evaluation may block another level, stage, or cycle.
