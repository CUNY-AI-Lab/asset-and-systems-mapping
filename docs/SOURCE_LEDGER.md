1. **Current direct decisions** — the direct correction supplied on 2026-08-24 governs the Micro/Meso boundary: Micro includes direct, within-setting relationships and their verbs; Meso contains noun-labeled linkages among at least two carried Micro settings and never generic verb edges.
2. **Earlier direct decisions** — the Maria and Zach Zoom material supplied on 2026-08-21 continues to govern the four analytical level names, their order, and the filterable ecological-layer map where it does not conflict with the later correction.

4. **Interaction and visual references** — the supplied local HTML wireframe and minimal DOCX define the staged experience and visual restraint where they remain compatible.
5. **Project framing** — the CALI knowledge-base document defines Maria Mavrides Calderon's teaching context and a memo-driven mapping process.
6. **Provisional meeting evidence** — the August 5 systems/assets notes are working minutes authored by Zach and remain unconfirmed by Maria.
7. **Architecture precedent** — the canonical STEM Splitter checkout informs hosting, model contracts, persistence, privacy, and validation. It does not determine this product's subject matter or visual metaphor.

Later direct user decisions supersede earlier specifications. The current progression is Micro, Meso, Exosphere, and Macrosphere. Micro identifies discrete elements in immediate settings and records their direct, within-setting relationships, including the relationship verbs. Meso names linkages among at least two carried Micro settings; it does not contain generic relationship edges or verbs. Exosphere replaces Macro; Macrosphere replaces Integration. The latter two levels use nested, filterable ecological regions. Instructor review stays advisory, and no review result or outstanding evaluation may block another level, stage, or cycle.
