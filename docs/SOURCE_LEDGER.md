# Source ledger

This ledger records what informed the local systems-and-resource mapping suite, how much authority each source carries, and what was deliberately excluded. It is an implementation aid, not a claim that every note was approved by every participant.

## Authority order

1. **Governing product specification** — the fuller Drive `memo-mapping.docx` is the most complete requirements source.
2. **Interaction and visual references** — the supplied local HTML wireframe and minimal DOCX define the intended staged experience and visual restraint.
3. **Project framing** — the CALI knowledge-base document defines Maria Mavrides Calderon's teaching context and a mapping process grounded in the memo.
4. **Provisional meeting evidence** — the August 5 systems/assets notes are working minutes authored by Zach and remain unconfirmed by Maria.
5. **Architecture precedent** — the canonical STEM Splitter checkout informs local hosting, model-contract, persistence, preflight, privacy, and validation patterns; it does not determine this product's subject-matter behavior or visual metaphor.

When sources conflict, the fuller Drive specification governs product behavior; the local wireframe governs presentation where it does not contradict that specification. Direct user decisions supersede both. The product is named **Systems Mapping**, uses a monochrome-dominant palette and restrained fieldwork/editorial typography, and names stages descriptively without invented narrative framing. The Teacher Console provides separate Maria and Zach accounts plus stage-specific guidance and course-material fields. The latest direct decisions establish a five-stage learning cycle around a persistent map: node selection and connection prompting share one stage; Part 1 groups Micro and Meso; Part 2 groups Macro and Integration; and instructor review remains asynchronous and advisory without controlling navigation or map access. A language model offers editable connection suggestions, each scale accumulates as a filterable layer, and students may revisit stages throughout a semester or year. Student interpretation remains secondary to assigned, published, institutional, public, and scholarly sources. Provisional minutes may motivate an open question but cannot override an authoritative source.
